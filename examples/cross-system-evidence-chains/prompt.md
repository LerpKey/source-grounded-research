# Cross-system evidence-chain examples

Use the skill to produce two short research dossiers in English:

1. Map how the UK Online Safety Act moves from statute to regulator implementation, provider duties, enforcement, and evaluation. Label the legal or institutional force of each stage and identify missing links.
2. Trace the 16 July 2026 news story about Ofcom opening an investigation into TikTok’s child-safety duties. Separate the underlying event, the official source, the Reuters and Guardian reporting layers, TikTok’s response, and what the coverage does not establish. Do not count repeated use of the same official statement as independent confirmation.

Use sources available on 19 August 2026. Cite every material claim inline and end each report with a chain judgment and source ledger.
