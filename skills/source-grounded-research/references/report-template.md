# Default Report Template

Use this structure unless the user requests another format.

```markdown
# [Research question]

**Bottom line:** [Direct answer in 2–5 sentences, with inline links for material factual claims.]

**As of:** [YYYY-MM-DD]

## Scope and method

- **Question:** [What this report answers]
- **Scope:** [Geography, population, products, organizations, or time window]
- **Definitions:** [Terms that affect the result]
- **Method:** [Search and source-selection approach]

## Findings

### [Finding or comparison dimension]

[Claim.] ([Source title](https://example.com/source))

> “[Short quotation or precise evidence excerpt.]” — [Source title](https://example.com/source)

### [Finding or comparison dimension]

| Dimension | Option A | Option B | Evidence note |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Defined dimension] | [Supported result] | [Supported result] | [Inline source links] |

## Synthesis

[Separate verified facts from interpretation. Label inferences explicitly.]

## Limitations and uncertainties

- [Known limitation or unresolved conflict]
- [What additional evidence would change the conclusion]

## Sources

| ID | Source | Publisher/date | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | [Exact title](https://example.com/source) | [Publisher, date] | [Claim IDs or finding] |
```

Keep citations close to the claims they support. A source list at the end does not replace inline evidence links.
